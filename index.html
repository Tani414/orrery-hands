Hand-controlled Orrery

Camera on the left, solar system on the right. Allow camera access and hold a hand up in good light.

  • ☝️ Point at a planet and hold for a moment: fly to it
  • 🤏 Pinch empty space and drag: rotate the view
  • 🤏 Pinch a planet and drag it left onto your camera: it floats in front of you. Drag it back right to return it
  • 🙌 Pinch with both hands, then spread or close: zoom, or resize a floating planet
  • ✊ Hold a fist: back to the solar system
  • ✌️ Hold a peace sign: next planet
  • 🖐 Hold an open palm: pause or resume time
